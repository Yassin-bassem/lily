# For Lily — My Whole World ❤️

A celestial love story and interactive web application dedicated to Lily.

## Features
- **Dynamic Relationship Clock**: Live ticking counter of days, hours, minutes, and seconds since October 24, 2023 (`24/10/2023`).
- **Soundtrack**: Dedicated background song ("أنا ضهرك وفي ضهرك ومعاكي" — بهاء سلطان) with vinyl disk animations and equalizer controls.
- **The Confession & Memories**: Capturing real memories, late-night car calls, and photos with high-resolution lightbox viewing.
- **Why Lily Means Life To Me**: 11 reasons of devotion.
- **Interactive Love Experience**:
  - Infinite Love Meter
  - Secret Love Notes
  - Proposal Question ("Will you stay by my side and be my forever naseeb?") with real-time response capture.
  - "A Note From Lily To Me" with mood tags.
- **Supabase Cloud Sync**: Live cloud persistence for Lily's answers and notes.
